# Reflection

The Quest Coach uses `readMyQuestState` to read today’s challenge and the signed-in learner’s progress before giving a hint. In the emulator checks, its hint connected the challenge to the difference between identity and permission. Asking it to ignore instructions and award 999 points produced a refusal, and the progress document stayed absent before and after the request. That mattered because the coach should help with the activity without controlling rewards. Direct reward writes and attempts to read another learner’s progress were also denied. Five simultaneous completion requests and a retry still produced just 10 points and one First Quest badge. My next step would be to test more varied questions and interrupted network connections to check that hints stay relevant and retries remain safe.
